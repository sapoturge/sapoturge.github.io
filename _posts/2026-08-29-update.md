Last time, I said I would either merge the next feature branch or write a post about it in a week.

It has been more than a week.

## Analysis of the problem

The purpose of the deadline was to get me to stop procrastinating merging finished features. That
has been an issue for most of **Froggum**'s recent development, along with having difficulty making
myself work on it at all. I specifically set the deadline to be: merge if it's finished, write
about it if it's not, so I would have no excuse not to do *something* the next week.

Turns out, there was another alternative: be reasonably sure that I could finish it that day, thus
allowing me to merge instead of post, then not manage to do either.

Missing the deadline turned out to be even worse for my motivation, so very little development has
happened in the last nine months since the deadline passed. 

## The solution

Part 1: I now have a dedicated space for being productive, whether that is working on **Froggum**,
remote work, or other productive things.

Part 2: I got a new laptop for working, which is permanently set in my productive space.

Part 3: Weekly progress updates.

In the past, posts have been mostly focused on finishing old features and planning new ones. I will
continue to do those posts, as they are very helpful in making **Froggum** actually work, and in
determining when a feature is done. In addition to those posts, there will be a weekly post
describing what happened that week in development, or something else interesting if no development
happened. These will be posted every Saturday, whether or not a new feature was implemented that
week, thus avoiding the issue of avoiding it by finishing a feature.
